# SingCoach development

Read `docs/rebuild-overview.md` and the current note at the top of `docs/project-memory.md`. Historical source paths in the handoff describe the former prototype; this rebuild uses `src/`.

Keep the app local, native, and small. Prioritize attacks, transitions, and repeatability. Show one correction per attempt, and abstain when evidence is unreliable. Pitch never identifies physiology, comfort, voice type, or register. At the user’s explicit request, show an “Estimate” field comparing matched range endpoints to typical reference spans, clearly labeled as a rough range-only comparison rather than a confirmed voice type; allow ambiguous/multiple-range results. Reference audio uses one generated piano-style sound for drills and range checks, as requested by the user. Keep it simple: no instrument selector, synthetic voice, or vocal formant logic. Range guides gently repeat piano notes so they remain audible.

Audio callbacks must not allocate, lock, log, do I/O, or run analysis. Preserve explicit Windows-direct audio under WSL; never silently fall back to WSLg. Keep reference and microphone evidence separate; range saved-note review shows actual captured matched holds in blue with a faint continuous purple guide-note indicator only (preserve voice timing and chart behavior), starts saving only at confirmation (excluding approach slides), trims trailing silence on release, hides empty/unmatched prompts, and keeps the live Compact pane rolling through singing pauses, with measured note labels and gaps for unreliable data, while reference guides remain explicitly distinct data and never fill microphone gaps, and require current playback readiness before guided range confirmation. Range starts with brief voice-only discovery and confirms each guide with one recent window (about one second, median within ±90 cents, at least 80% reliable frames and 80% of reliable pitch within the same ±90-cent green band). Brief wobbles are allowed and no silence or second confirmation is required after failed or unclear attempts. Guided pacing adds one second to hear the piano before singing and analysis. After matching, stay on Hold as long as the singer continues; 120 ms without reliable voiced input releases the note (quiet or unvoiced background noise, excluding clipping). There is no minimum singing-slot wait after release, followed by an automatic one-second breathing pause. Record the longest reliable in-band sustain at the highest matched note as a session fun metric, not a physiological measure. Direction changes have a two-second pause. Exercise reference notes last two seconds each; scoring thresholds stay unchanged. Retries are welcome; misses may reduce the step to one semitone but never infer a reach limit. Reliable sustained singing above the initial baseline postpones automatic descent, even when off target; allow six seconds after the last such evidence before applying the timeout, but cap an unmatched target at twelve ready seconds (one extra set of three repetitions). Matched voluntary holds are exempt from this timeout. Stop piano audio after three repetitions (six ready seconds) per range guide, but continue microphone tracking of an already matched voluntary hold until release; do not require continuing audio for that hold. New guided confirmations still require current ready playback. After three unanswered ready piano repetitions (six seconds) while climbing, automatically descend from the highest matched note without counting the unanswered target or calling it a vocal limit. During descent, three unanswered ready repetitions lower the guide again while returning at or above the initial baseline; below that baseline they finish with matched notes only instead of continuing toward C2. Successful low matches may continue descending to the supported C2 bound. Above-baseline protection applies only to the climb. Keep a permanent compact instruction cue at the top right, 60 px high (twice the Find my range button), with icon/short action text; show target/live sustain where relevant and supporting note/sustain/guidance underneath and full stage/last-matched information on hover. Keep the completed high/low result and highest sustain visible in the header summary. The user can also choose to descend or finish; supported C2/C6 bounds still apply. Silence and unreliable input never establish a limit. Bound session history.

Run locked/offline Cargo tests, Clippy with warnings denied, formatting checks, and a release build for implementation changes. Record hardware checks separately from automated success. Preserve historical reference documents and update the current handoff when making meaningful changes.
